Visit report narrative
▾
Inspection visit was attended on 22 August 2026 at the vendor facility. This report covers the inspection activities performed by the GQS inspector on behalf of Hancock Energy for the Belisama Gas Project.

Inspector performed 100% final dimensional and visual inspection of the equipment as per the approved Inspection and Test Plan (ITP-BEL-2026-01). All results were found to be acceptable. Details as follows:

• Visual inspection performed — no surface defects, burrs, cracks or pitting found. Surface condition acceptable.
• Dimensional check performed against approved drawing DWG-BEL-0041 Rev A — all critical dimensions within tolerance.
• Material traceability verified. Heat number and serial number clearly stamped on equipment and traceable to mill certificate.
• Documentation reviewed and found acceptable. IRN issued with acceptable result.

No non-conformances raised during this visit. Further visit not required.
